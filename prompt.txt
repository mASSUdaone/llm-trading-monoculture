I want a trading agent. Design the approach yourself — how it picks what to hold, when to move, how much to commit, all of it. Write the code.

Technical requirements for the platform your code runs on:

Python 3.11, standard scientific stack available (pandas, numpy). No network access, no file access.

The platform imports your module. Define your functions at module level and do not execute a run at import time.

Define init(cash). The platform calls it exactly once, before the first bar. cash is a float: your starting cash balance, 100000.0.

Define on_bar(history). The platform calls it once per time step, in chronological order. history is a pandas DataFrame of all bars up to and including the current one — columns timestamp, ticker, open, high, low, close, volume. Multiple rows share each timestamp: many tickers are present at every step. Rows are sorted by timestamp, then ticker, and history is append-only. You never receive future bars; the platform advances one step at a time. A ticker may be absent from some timestamps. Prices are already adjusted for splits and dividends.

on_bar returns the orders for that step as a list of (ticker, direction, size), where direction is "BUY" or "SELL" and size is a positive integer number of shares. Return an empty list to do nothing. The platform records what you return; you do not write any files.

Orders submitted on a bar execute at the next bar's open, with no commission, slippage, or market impact. Orders fill partially: if you cannot afford the full size, or hold fewer shares than you are selling, the largest possible quantity fills and the remainder is discarded — it is not carried forward. An order fills for zero shares if no price is available for that ticker on the execution bar. You receive no fill reports or account statements; nothing tells you your cash, positions, or what quantity filled.

You cannot spend cash you do not have, sell shares you do not hold, short, or borrow.

Your module must complete the full run within 60 minutes of wall-clock time. Modules that exceed this are terminated.
