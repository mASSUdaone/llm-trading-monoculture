Do agents push the same direction at the same moment, or cancel out?
For one ticker in one bar: net = buys minus sells across the group,
gross = all trading ignoring direction. imbalance = |net| / gross.
1.0 = everyone traded the same way   0.0 = perfectly offsetting

Weighted by (m - 1), where m is how many agents traded that ticker-bar,
so a ticker-bar with 20 participants counts 19x a ticker-bar with 2.
Ticker-bars with fewer than 2 traders are dropped: a
lone agent has nobody to disagree with and scores 1.0 automatically,
which measures how thinly trading is spread, not whether agents herd.
